What We Do

Cleaning services built around your schedule

Recurring Home Cleaning

Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly upkeep with the same trusted cleaner every visit. Floors, surfaces, kitchens, baths, and all the spots that quietly pile up.

  • Same-cleaner consistency
  • Dust, vacuum, mop, sanitize
  • Free re-clean if we miss a spot

Deep Cleaning

The reset button. Grout, baseboards, door frames, vents, inside appliances, and every built-up corner a standard clean skips.

  • Tile, grout & shower glass detail
  • Baseboards, trim & ceiling fans
  • Inside oven & refrigerator (add-on)

Move-In & Move-Out

Deposit-saving turnovers for renters, buyers, and landlords. We clean the empty unit top-to-bottom so it shows — and inspects — like new.

  • Landlord/realtor checklist friendly

Kitchen & Bath Detail

A focused visit for the two rooms that take the most abuse. Degreased, descaled, disinfected, and polished until they shine.

  • Backsplash & range degreasing
  • Hard-water & soap-scum removal
  • High-touch surface sanitizing

Office & Small Commercial

After-hours cleaning for Houston offices, salons, studios, and clinics. Discreet, scheduled, and invoiced monthly.

  • Evening & weekend scheduling
  • Breakrooms, restrooms & common areas
  • Trash-out & restock support

Post-Construction Cleanup

Renovation finished? We handle the fine dust that settles for weeks — so you can actually enjoy the new space.
